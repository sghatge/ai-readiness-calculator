# Overview of **AI Readiness Calculator**

## What Is **AI Readiness Calculator**?
**AI Readiness Calculator** is a free **web application** that measures how ready an employee is to use AI in their daily work. A short self-assessment (31 questions across 10 dimensions) produces a **0–100 readiness score**, a readiness level, strengths, gaps and a personal 30-day plan. Team leads can combine results into a team view with discussion cards.

## Why Use **AI Readiness Calculator**?
- **Problem it addresses:** AI adoption often stalls because organizations cannot see where people are blocked, whether by skills, motivation, tool access or safe data handling.
- **Purpose:** Give individuals and managers a consistent, structured way to measure AI readiness and decide on the next action.
- **Key Benefits:**
  - **Covers more than skills:** includes mindset, role fit, access, and **data privacy and responsible use** (based on ADKAR, OECD and NIST AI RMF).
  - **Flags critical risks**, such as unsafe data handling, which caps the score until it is fixed.
  - **Turns results into action:** a 30-day plan, suggested training and team discussion cards.

### Typical Use Cases
- **Individual baseline:** take the assessment and track progress in the **History** tab.
- **Team workshop:** a facilitator collects anonymous result codes and reviews the team profile together.
- **Enablement planning:** use dimension averages and the 90-day roadmap to prioritize training.

## How to Access **AI Readiness Calculator**
- **Access Method:** Web browser; nothing to install.

- **Supported Platforms:** Current desktop and mobile browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
- **System Requirements:** JavaScript enabled; local storage allowed if you want to keep history.
- **Sign-in / Permissions:** None. No account required.

## Who Can Benefit?
- **Primary Audience:** Employees in any function who want to understand and improve how they use AI at work.
- **Secondary Users:** Team leads, workshop facilitators, and L&D or AI-enablement teams planning training and rollout.
- **Not intended for:** Performance appraisal, hiring or other employment decisions. It is a self-assessment for development only.

## Data & Privacy
- **Data collected:** Profile choices and answers, scored in the browser. Anonymous mode is on by default.
- **Where data is stored:** Only in the user's own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to a server.
- **Sharing:** Users choose to share via CSV, a PDF report, a share image, or a workshop code (anonymous ID, department and scores only).

## Getting Started Checklist
